How to play

  • 🔎

    The seeker types questions to everyone, then gets one guess at who the hider is.

  • 🙈

    The hider has to answer every question truthfully, without getting caught.

  • 🎭

    The fakies know who the hider is. They can lie to confuse the seeker and protect the hider.

3 or more players, each on their own device. Roles are picked at random and kept secret.
